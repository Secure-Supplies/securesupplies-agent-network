## Notes

- **nano bubble water fuel** is a fixed technical term of this network. In all five languages it is translated word-for-word as in the table — never shortened to "water fuel", and never replaced with a local marketing phrase.
- **DEF** keeps the abbreviation "DEF" and the standard name "AUS 32" in every language, with the expansion translated once in brackets on first use.
- The giveaway rule is translated in full wherever it appears: the local equivalent of "$1 spent = 1 entry. Free entry / no purchase necessary."
- Translated documents live in `i18n/drafts/` until a native speaker has reviewed them against this glossary and the English source.
